About CO2 Counter

Your footprint, counted honestly. Then made smaller.

CO2 Counter is a free, six-step survey that works out your yearly carbon footprint from official UK data, then shows you the changes that cut it the most.

  • Free for everyone
  • Independent
  • Official UK data
  • Kept going by supporters

What we believe

  1. 01

    Count it honestly.

    Real UK factors, shown openly. No guilt, and no inflated numbers to scare you into acting.

  2. 02

    Make the next step obvious.

    A number alone changes nothing. We point to the few changes that matter most for you.

  3. 03

    Stay on your side.

    Nobody pays to be ranked higher. Suggestions are picked for the climate, not for a fee.

Where the numbers come from

Official data in. Your estimate out.

It's an estimate, not a meter reading. The factors are UK averages, so your real footprint will differ a little. It's accurate enough to show where your biggest savings are.
