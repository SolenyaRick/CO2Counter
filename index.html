Pets Optional

Uses rough average footprints of ~770 kg CO2e/yr per dog and ~310 kg CO2e/yr per cat — mostly driven by their (often meat-heavy) diet — split evenly across everyone in your household (the "People in your household" figure above), the same way home energy is, since a household's pets aren't really just one person's footprint. Answering either one counts the other as 0 if left blank.

Banking Optional

Uses MotherTree's bank carbon emissions league table, which estimates each UK bank's indirect footprint from financing fossil fuels, per £ of customer deposits held. Both the bank and a balance need answering for this to count — picking a bank without a balance (or vice versa) leaves it out of your total, same as any other optional field here.

Buying goods

Uses a rough blended average of ~10 kg CO2e per item — a T-shirt is much less, a coat or a pair of jeans much more.

  • Travel beyond your logged commute: taxis/rideshare, buses, trains, ferries, cruises

  • Investments beyond your bank balance — stocks, funds, and pensions can carry their own financed-emissions footprint (will be available soon...)

Vehicle Optional

If yes, adds a rough ~700 kg CO2e/yr for the car's own manufacturing footprint, amortized over an average ~14-year car lifetime — separate from the fuel/charging for trips you log elsewhere in the app.

Uses rough DEFRA-style factors: diesel ~0.171 kg CO2e/km (about the same as the blended average used if you leave this blank), hybrid ~0.111 kg CO2e/km, electric ~0.058 kg CO2e/km (using average UK grid intensity to charge it). Daily reminder

Only available in the iOS app, not the web version — local notifications need the native app to actually schedule them.

A single local notification, rescheduled on this device whenever you open the app — nothing is sent from a server, and it's always the same generic nudge, never your data. Stored on this device only, so you'll need to turn it back on if you reinstall the app or sign in elsewhere.

Baseline week

By default, the "Compared to an average week" card on the front page measures you against a bottom-up UK average. Pick one of your own fully confirmed weeks here instead to compare against that real week of yours going forward — only weeks with a “✓ FULL” badge on the History section show up here. Switch back to "UK average" any time.

Data sharing

Off by default — nothing is shared unless you turn this on. If enabled, everything on this page and the Stats page becomes visible to the app developer except your bank/banking answers, which are always left out. Your name, email, and account are never included — the developer only ever sees the raw numbers (commute distance, diet mix, flights, home energy, heating, pets, water, etc.), not who they belong to.

Only visible signed in as the app owner — pulls every opted-in user's data via a server-side check on your account email, same data as the research_profiles/research_weeks views in supabase/schema.sql. The Excel version loads a small spreadsheet library on demand (only when you click it), and splits the data into a "Profiles" and a "Weeks" sheet.
