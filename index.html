Flying

Counted per return trip: ~250 kg CO2e for a short-haul European flight, ~1,600 kg CO2e for a long-haul international one — rough averages, actual footprint varies a lot by distance and cabin class.

Home energy

Include everyday use like showering (if electric water heating), lighting, heating, and appliances — check a recent bill if unsure. Uses a rough grid average of ~0.2 kg CO2e/kWh, split evenly across everyone in the household.

Buying goods

Uses a rough blended average of ~10 kg CO2e per item — a T-shirt is much less, a coat or a pair of jeans much more.
