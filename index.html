Pets Optional

Uses rough average footprints of ~770 kg CO2e/yr per dog and ~310 kg CO2e/yr per cat — mostly driven by their (often meat-heavy) diet — split evenly across everyone in your household (the "People in your household" figure above), the same way home energy is, since a household's pets aren't really just one person's footprint. Answering either one counts the other as 0 if left blank.

Banking Optional

Uses MotherTree's bank carbon emissions league table, which estimates each UK bank's indirect footprint from financing fossil fuels, per £ of customer deposits held. Both the bank and a balance need answering for this to count — picking a bank without a balance (or vice versa) leaves it out of your total, same as any other optional field here.

Buying goods

Uses a rough blended average of ~10 kg CO2e per item — a T-shirt is much less, a coat or a pair of jeans much more.

What this doesn't account for

  • Travel beyond your logged commute: taxis/rideshare, buses, trains, ferries, cruises
  • Diet + purchases beyond what's tracked: dairy, eggs, snacks, food miles, electronics, subscriptions, household waste
  • Investments beyond your bank balance — stocks, funds, and pensions can carry their own financed-emissions footprint (will be available soon...)
  • Your share of public services and infrastructure — roughly 3.3 tonnes CO2e/yr per person in the UK, and real, but not something you can meaningfully reduce or self-report here

Profile

"Match UK average week" sets your goal to the same commute + food UK average shown on the Stats page (only those two, since that's what this weekly goal tracks). "1.5°C 2030 (food + commute)" is our own estimate — applying published 2030 reduction requirements for developed countries (nutrition −47%, mobility −72%, per Hot or Cool Institute research). "Match world average week" is the roughest of the three — rough estimate assuming less car use and less meat than the UK figures.

Wasted food still carries the emissions it took to produce, so this scales up your food figures everywhere (This Week, Stats) to account for it — e.g. 10–30% waste means buying/producing about 25% more than you actually eat. Your goal colors the boxes on the Weeks page: green at or under goal, amber a bit over, red well over.

Baseline week

By default, the "Compared to an average week" card on the front page measures you against a bottom-up UK average. Pick one of your own fully confirmed weeks here instead to compare against that real week of yours going forward — only weeks with a “✓ FULL” badge on the Weeks page show up here. Switch back to "UK average" any time.
