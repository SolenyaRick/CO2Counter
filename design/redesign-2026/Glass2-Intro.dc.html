Why it matters

is roughly each person’s yearly share of CO₂e if we’re to keep warming to 1.5°C. Most of us in the UK are well above it, and small, repeated changes are how we close the gap.

Questions people ask

Do I need an account?

No. Take the survey as a guest. Create a free account if you want to save your results across devices.

How accurate is it?

It’s an estimate using typical lifecycle emission factors. It’s built to show your biggest levers clearly, not to be a precise audit.

What do I need to have to hand?

Nothing essential. An energy bill helps with the home step, and a rough idea of your savings and pension helps with money, but ballpark answers are fine.
