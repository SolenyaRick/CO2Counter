About us

Free, independent, built on official figures

CO2 Counter is a free carbon footprint calculator for people in the UK. It shows where your footprint really comes from, and the changes that would cut it most.

Free for everyone No adverts Never sells your data Not funded by any company

What it is

Answer six short steps about your food, travel, flights, home, pets and money. You get your yearly footprint, what it’s made of, and the changes that would cut it most.

It’s free for everyone. People who find it useful can support it for £2 a year.

Who runs it

An independent UK project run by Jack Brown. It isn’t owned or funded by any energy company, bank or other business.

CO2counter@outlook.com

Where the figures come from

UK government emission factors, official statistics and peer-reviewed research for food. We update them as new data is published.

How we choose partners

1

Climate first

Renewable electricity, banks that don’t finance fossil fuels, food that cuts waste and meat, and rail instead of flying.

2

Free options too

We list options that pay us nothing, like The Man in Seat 61 and Fork Ranger, alongside ones that might.

3

Figures never change

Being a partner never changes anyone’s footprint, savings or rankings. Bank and pension suggestions are never affiliate links.

Affiliate links are always marked with an Affiliate tag. At the moment, none of our links are affiliate links.
